📊 Sales Dashboard - Power BI

This repository contains a Sales Analysis Dashboard created in Power BI.
The dashboard is designed to analyze and visualize sales performance across multiple dimensions such as products, regions, customers, and trends.

🔧 Features

Total Sales, Profit & Avg. Order Analysis

Sales by Product Category (Bikes, Accessories, Clothing)

Sales by Age Group (Youth, Adults, Seniors)

Sales by Country & State

Yearly Sales Trend (2011 - 2016)

Top 5 & Bottom 5 Products by Quantity

Fully interactive filters/slicers: Month, Country, State, Sub-Category

📈 Insights Highlighted

Bikes are the leading product category contributing 62M+ sales.

The Adult (35-64) age group drives nearly 50% of sales.

The United States leads with 28M in sales, followed by Australia (21M).

Sales show steady growth with peaks in 2014 & 2016.

Accessories dominate in quantity sold, while bikes dominate in revenue.

🛠 Tools & Technologies

Power BI for data visualization & dashboard creation

Data Modeling with calculated measures (DAX)

Interactive Filtering for better drill-down analysis
